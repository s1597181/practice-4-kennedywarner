Serves 4 · 45 minutes

Slow BBQ Chicken

Low heat, a lid, and sauce added late so it never burns.

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken thighs, bone in
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 cup barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Heat the oven
  2. Season the chicken
  3. Bake covered
  4. Sauce and finish
  5. Rest and serve
